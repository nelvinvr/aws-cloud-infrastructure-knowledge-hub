## First Publishing Cycle

The first complete article will be **AWS Shared Responsibility Model Explained**.

It will follow this sequence:

1. Study official AWS documentation.
2. Explain the concept without notes.
3. Identify misunderstandings and open questions.
4. Complete a small practical responsibility-mapping exercise.
5. Write the article using the standard template.
6. Review technical accuracy, originality, links, and claims.
7. Publish through a branch and pull request.
8. Schedule a future freshness review.

## Review Policy

- Review service articles at least every 12 months.
- Review sooner when AWS announces a relevant change.
- Verify pricing, quotas, certification details, feature availability, and service names immediately before publication.
- Mark materially outdated pages with 🔄 until reviewed.
- Keep review dates inside every published article.

## Backlog Rules

1. Finish the current article before starting another public draft.
2. A LinkedIn post does not automatically count as a GitHub article.
3. A deployment does not count as a completed lab until it is validated and cleaned up.
4. A diagram must be original and checked against the written architecture.
5. Quality and accuracy take priority over publishing frequency.
